# Job + Freelance Hunter

Automated discovery for Junior Frontend React/TypeScript and HTML/CSS employment vacancies.

## Work location
- Russia: remote only.
- Yekaterinburg: remote or hybrid.
- All other cities: remote only.
- Office / on-site roles are excluded everywhere.
- International remote roles must explicitly allow candidates residing in Russia.

## Priority
Employment vacancies are always primary. Freelance projects are a fallback and are surfaced/processed only when no eligible employment vacancy is found. Paid bids, credits, and subscriptions are never used.

Protected application flows, CAPTCHA and 2FA are never bypassed.
